I've audited more Shopify stores than I can count at this point. Premium brands, scrappy startups, established retailers making the jump to DTC — they're all different, but the conversion killers are surprisingly consistent.

After running these audits dozens of times, I've distilled the process into a checklist. These **12 checks** catch roughly 80% of the revenue that's being left on the table.

## The Checklist

### Homepage & Navigation

- **Clear value proposition above the fold** — Can a first-time visitor understand what you sell and why it matters within 3 seconds? If your hero is a lifestyle image with no copy, you're losing people.
- **Navigation depth ≤ 2 clicks to product** — Every additional click is a dropout point. If someone has to go Home → Collections → Subcategory → Product, you've already lost 40% of them.
- **Search that actually works** — Test your own search. Type in a misspelling. Type in a colloquial name for your product. If it returns nothing, that's lost revenue.

### Product Pages

> Product pages are where the money is. If you only fix one thing after an audit, fix your product pages.

- **Real benefit-driven copy, not just specs** — Nobody buys a "100% organic cotton tee with reinforced stitching." They buy the softest shirt they've ever worn that lasts forever. Translate features into benefits.
- **Social proof near the CTA** — Reviews, ratings, user photos — they need to be *visible without scrolling past the add-to-cart button*. Not buried at the bottom.
- **Urgency without sleaze** — Real inventory counts, real shipping cutoffs. Never fake it. Customers can tell, and you'll destroy trust.

### Cart & Checkout

- **Cart upsells that make sense** — Don't recommend random products. If someone's buying a camera, suggest the memory card and the carrying case. Keep it to 1-2 items max.
- **Guest checkout option** — Requiring account creation before purchase is conversion suicide. Let people buy first, create accounts later.
- **Shipping cost transparency** — If you can't offer free shipping, show the cost *before* checkout. Surprise shipping fees are the #1 reason for cart abandonment.

### Post-Purchase

- **Order confirmation email within 60 seconds** — This is the highest-open-rate email you'll ever send. Make it count with cross-sells, referral asks, or brand storytelling.
- **Shipping notification with tracking** — Table stakes, but you'd be surprised how many stores skip this.
- **Review request at the right time** — Send it after the customer has had time to use the product, not the day it arrives.

## How I Score It

I use a simple scoring system for each item:

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 0 | Missing entirely |
| 1 | Present but needs significant improvement |
| 2 | Solid implementation |
| 3 | Best-in-class execution |

A perfect score is 36. Most stores I audit land between **12-18** on their first pass. Getting to 28+ is usually where you see a meaningful conversion lift.

## The Common Thread

If you look at this list, there's a theme: **remove friction and build trust**. Every item is either eliminating a reason *not* to buy or adding a reason *to* buy. That's really all conversion optimization is at its core.

The stores that win aren't necessarily the ones with the best products or the biggest budgets. They're the ones that make buying *easy* and make the customer feel *confident* about their purchase.

Start with the checklist. Fix the zeros first. Then work your way up.
